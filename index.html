3. Conditional Probability Table (KRR Rule Base)

The network encodes knowledge as a factoring joint distribution: P(C, I, E, A) = P(C)P(I)P(E)P(A | C, I, E). Priors are uniformly assumed here at 0.5 for demonstration.
